# Vibe spec

- **Place or object:** the night shift at a flight-dynamics desk: a printed conjunction report under a lamp.
- **One emotion in 3 seconds:** calm certainty. The operator knows what to do and why.
- **Two influences that collide:** a Swiss railway departure board (big, plain, tabular figures) and a
  mission-ops paper log (mono timestamps, hairline rules).
- **Never mistaken for:** a sci-fi HUD or a crypto dashboard. No glow, no gradients, no neon.

Derived: deep ink background, warm paper-white text, one state colour per decision (amber manoeuvre,
teal monitor), IBM Plex Sans for words and IBM Plex Mono for every number, hairlines instead of boxes.
