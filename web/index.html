KAKSHA · COLLISION RISK CONSOLE

Two days before closest approach: plan a manoeuvre, or keep monitoring?

For each close approach in ESA's held-out set, KAKSHA reads only the warning messages received at least two days out, forecasts the final collision risk, and recommends what to do, with its confidence and the reasons.

Held-out events

    Fuel against risk: move the decision line

    Lower the probability needed to recommend a manoeuvre and fewer dangerous events are missed, but more manoeuvres are spent on events that end safe. Counts are over all 2,167 held-out events. The marked operating point is the line each fold chose from its own training events.

    Is the confidence honest?

    Held-out events grouped by the probability KAKSHA gave. If it says 0.6, about 60 % of those events should end high risk.
